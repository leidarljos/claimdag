* How-to guides

These are the tasks after the [[file:getting-started.org][tutorial]]. Each one
is a single goal. The [[file:concepts.org][concepts]] page defines the words,
and the [[file:reference.org][reference]] is every flag.

- [[file:howto-dir.org][Point at a graph]]: =--dir=, =CLAIMDAG_DIR=, and the defaults =where= prints.
- [[file:howto-take.org][Take the next node]]: =ready=, =ready --balanced=, and =claim-next=.
- [[file:howto-lease.org][Fence a completion]]: =renew=, =release=, =reclaim=, and =complete --gen=.
- [[file:howto-archive.org][Archive finished work]]: =archive=, =list --terminal=, =list --all=.
- [[file:howto-pane.org][Drive the pane]]: keys, =--dump=, =CLAIMDAG_ACTOR=, =CLAIMDAG_HANDLES=.
- [[file:howto-mcp.org][Serve the graph]]: the tools and the two prompts.

A tracker id is mapped by the seat, documented at https://leidarljos.github.io.
On its own, claimdag takes 32-hex ids only.
