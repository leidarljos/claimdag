* Tutorials

The tutorial is one path. It uses a scratch directory, two nodes, and one
actor. By the end a second claim has been refused, a lease has been
renewed, and finishing the parent has made the child ready.

Put =claimdag= on =PATH= from the [[file:install.org][installation]] page,
then follow [[file:getting-started.org][Getting started]] from the first
command. The page is the whole lesson. The [[file:howto.org][how-to guides]]
are the tasks that come after it, and the [[file:concepts.org][concepts]] page
names the edges the lesson creates.
