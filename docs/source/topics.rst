Topics
======

Topics are the background for someone who has run the
:doc:`tutorial <getting-started>`.

-  :doc:`Concepts <concepts>` draws a node, the parent edge, the dependency edge, and the generation.
-  :doc:`Explanation <explanation>` is why a claim expires and why ``complete --gen`` refuses a stale holder.
-  :doc:`Architecture <architecture>` is the snapshot file, the 4096-node cap, and which crate holds what.
-  :doc:`In the seat <seat>` is the other crates a session reads, and the sites that document them.

The :doc:`reference <reference>` and the :doc:`API reference <api>`
are the inventories. The :doc:`how-to guides <howto>` are the tasks.
